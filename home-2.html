The Process

From Artwork to Finished Frame

A collaborative approach to ensuring your piece is protected and presented beautifully.

We believe that every framing project is a partnership between our artisans and your vision. It requires patience, precision, and a deep understanding of materials.

01

Bring Your Piece

We examine the artwork, discuss its significance, and take precise measurements.

02

Explore Options

Together, we review frame styles, mat combinations, and glazing materials.

03

Refine The Design

Finalizing proportions and ensuring all conservation requirements are met.

04

Craft The Frame

Our artisans cut, join, and assemble the frame in our studio with meticulous attention to detail.

05

Ready To Display

The finished piece is carefully sealed, wired, and prepared for your wall.
